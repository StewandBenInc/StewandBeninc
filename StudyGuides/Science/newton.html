Newton Unit

      Formulas

    • Speed = Distance ÷ Time
    • Velocity = Distance ÷ Time (with direction)
    • Δv (Change in velocity) = Final velocity − Initial velocity
    • Δt (Change in time) = Final time − Initial time
    • Acceleration = Δv ÷ Δt
    • Force = Mass × Acceleration

      Newton's Laws

    • First Law (Inertia): Objects stay at rest or in motion unless acted on by an outside force.
    • Second Law: Force = Mass × Acceleration (F = ma)
    • Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

      Graphing

    • A complete line or bar graph includes:
    • A descriptive title
    • Labeled x-axis and y-axis (with units)
    • Evenly spaced intervals
    • Key/legend (if more than one set of data is shown)
    • Neat and accurate data plotting
