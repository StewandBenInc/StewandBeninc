What We Do

We provide helpful study guides (made by us and by members of the community), an English vocab dictionary, weather updates powered by NOAA(which is recieving less funding now so weather accuracy may not bet the best), and even a grade-wide calendar for tests and birthdays. Oh, and if you know where to look... we have some hidden games too.

Why We Built This

We got tired of juggling a million apps and documents just to survive the school week. So we built something better — a one-stop spot to find everything from vocab words to tomorrow’s test schedule. Also, we hate having our games get blocked ;).

Community Contributions

This project runs on good vibes and good notes. If you’ve made a killer study guide, submit it and help everyone out. We’ll credit you (unless you want to stay anonymous).

Just a Heads Up

We’re not pros — this is a student-run site, so expect the occasional bug or meme. We’re learning as we go.

Want to Get Involved?

Whether you’ve got ideas, questions, or content to share, we’d love to hear from you. Shoot us a message at stewandbeninc@gmail.com or use our Feedback Form.
